Profile-based guidance

Personalized University Shortlisting

Send us your department name, academic profile and target, and we will help identify universities and programs that are a stronger match for you.

What you can send

  • CV / academic profile
  • Transcript and grades
  • Bachelor’s course list or syllabus
  • IELTS / MOI / GRE details, if applicable
  • Target subject, intake, budget and preferences
  • Your own university list, if you already have one

What we will help with

  • Find relevant profile-matched programs
  • Compare major admission requirements
  • Highlight stronger, moderate and ambitious matches
  • Point out programs that may not fit your background
  • Help with related-subject university lists as well

Available for a very small support fee. Contact us first with what you need and we will discuss the scope with you.

This is not an agency or admission consultancy service !!! The recommendations are meant to help you identify suitable universities and save time during your research. Admission is never guaranteed, and final eligibility always depends on the university’s official requirements and decision.
